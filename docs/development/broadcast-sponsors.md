# Draft layouts and sponsor slideshow

All new draft themes render the shared sponsor playlist on a 1920x1080 transparent canvas. Their compositions are intentionally different:

- Classic retains the original 1920x367 orange/red/white display2 strip, 725px team banks and a 470px center console. Sponsors occupy its upper-center banner.
- Studio uses a 1920x344 white broadcast desk: wider 840px team banks, a slim 240px clock/sponsor column, team logos/names/scores in the team headers, and match/game information near the bottom of the center column. There is no gold trapezoid.
- Arena uses a 1920x454 navy/cyan tournament stage, including raised team-branding panels. Subtly mirrored frames with upright artwork occupy two broad 952px banks with a narrow dark divider. Team logos/names, small series-win indicators, one draft clock and match/phase information occupy the top header. Bans, coaches and the sponsor slot occupy the footer. The latest reference uses a mild mirrored slant: +4 degrees on the left and -4 degrees on the right. Artwork is counter-skewed and player bars remain horizontal. There is no white wedge or duplicate timer.

All retain coach, hero, lane, score, phase, clock, team logo/name and player bindings. No BPM or authored league logo is introduced. Original legacy display URLs stay fixed.

Open `/broadcast-themes.html`, find **Sponsor slideshow**, and click **Preview draft** to inspect the currently selected theme. Add one PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF at a time (3 MiB per logo, up to 20). Uploads save immediately. Reorder/remove, change the 2–60-second interval or toggle display, then click **Save slideshow**. Save pending edits before uploading another logo. A single logo holds; multiple logos rotate in saved order. Draft and scoreboard use a shared 800ms right-to-left pan transition, with the configured interval spent holding each logo after the transition. A clipped inner viewport keeps moving logos inside their existing sponsor slot. The outgoing decoded logo remains until the next logo loads; reduced-motion preferences switch directly. Playlist changes and page lifecycle events cancel pending animation and retain only the current image. The slot stays blank when disabled or empty. Logo images use contain sizing and preserve transparency. Previewing a theme does not activate it globally; the existing theme activation button still controls the selected broadcast theme.

Settings are global to all new draft themes, including normal and six-ban formats, and independent of match state. Roster and legacy display outputs do not render this sponsor slot. Legacy roster sponsor settings remain separate. Changes made here update all open draft sources after acknowledgement.

`GET /api/broadcast-sponsors` returns `{data,revision}`. `POST /api/broadcast-sponsors/logos` accepts multipart field `logo`. `PUT /api/broadcast-sponsors` accepts `{enabled,intervalSeconds,logoIds}` for ordering/removal. Writes require the latest `If-Match` revision and return 409 for stale operator settings. State is committed atomically before `broadcast_sponsors_update` is sent over the existing WebSocket. Failed uploads/commits do not publish a playlist and clean up their unpublished file.

Runtime files are `runtime/broadcast-sponsors.json` and `runtime/sponsor-logos/`, both ignored by Git. Back up both together. Startup validates saved settings and does not silently replace a corrupt file. Logo uploads use generated names, raster-signature checks, and a size limit; SVG/HTML uploads are not accepted. Removed files are deleted after the settings commit; an unlink failure does not undo an acknowledged save. This tool's existing local/LAN access model remains unchanged.

The renderer fetches settings on mount/recovery, consumes the existing draft socket's sponsor events, and polls every five seconds for recovery. Playlist changes preserve the current logo when possible. A failed logo retains the last decoded logo and advances on the next interval; a failed initial logo leaves the slot blank. It never writes match, draft or sponsor state. Settings and playlist order survive restart; slide elapsed time does not.

Verification uses isolated data, real upload and persistence routes, browser decoding, rotation, reorder, enable/disable, stale-editor rejection, recovery, and identical draft state before/after sponsor edits. Browser composition is checked with synthetic artwork across all three themes and both ban formats, including visible sponsor bounds, portrait alignment, gently mirrored Arena frames with upright art/labels, separate player-name bars, and three distinct bank/center arrangements. Actual Windows/OBS rendering, font availability and final sponsor assets require checking on the production PC.

Selected hero artwork replaces the player portrait in a draft slot, including during hero-stat cards. Clearing/resetting a slot restores its portrait. This presentation rule is shared by the new themes and primary legacy display2 pages. Large numeric Arena series scores are removed; the stored scores and small win indicators are retained.
